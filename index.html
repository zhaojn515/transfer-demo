Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to enter China?
It depends on your nationality. Many countries are eligible for 144-hour visa-free transit in Shenzhen. Your driver will provide guidance on visa policies and assist with entry forms.
What if my flight is delayed?
We monitor flight statuses in real-time. If your flight is delayed, we'll adjust the pickup time accordingly. For Standard service, there's no extra charge for reasonable delays. Premium service includes 30 minutes of complimentary waiting time.
How do I pay for the service?
We accept credit card (via secure online payment), Alipay, WeChat Pay, and cash (HKD/RMB/USD). Payment details will be sent after booking confirmation.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking up to 24 hours before the pickup time for a full refund. Changes within 24 hours may incur a fee. Contact us via WhatsApp for assistance.
Is the price per person or per vehicle?
The price is per vehicle, not per person. You can book for 1-7 passengers depending on the vehicle. All passengers in your group will travel together in the same vehicle.
